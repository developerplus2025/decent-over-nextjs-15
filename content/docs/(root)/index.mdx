# Welcome to the LibreNote App Documentation

This documentation contains everything you need to install, explore, and master the LibreNote App.  
From beginners to advanced users, you will find step-by-step guides, feature explanations, and troubleshooting help.

---

## What You’ll Find Here

- **Getting Started** – How to install the app, set it up, and start playing music.  
- **Audio Effects** – Detailed guides on sound enhancements, mixing tools, and equalizer settings.  
- **Playback Features** – Learn to use repeat, shuffle, queue, and playback speed controls.  
- **Library Management** – Organize your music with playlists, tags, and search filters.  
- **Settings Customization** – Change the interface, apply themes, and configure shortcuts.  
- **Advanced Usage** – Unlock features for DJs, sound engineers, and power users.  
- **API Reference** – Technical details for integrating the app with your own projects.  
- **Troubleshooting** – Solutions for common issues.

---

## Who This Guide Is For

- Users who want to enjoy music without technical complexity.  
- Musicians and creators who want control over audio effects and music organization.  
- Developers looking to integrate the app with external systems.
